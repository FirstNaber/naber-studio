02 — RepRally · Texas

RepRally

A new territory with zero accounts, built into a recurring wholesale book.

Read the story+

I was one of RepRally’s first reps in Texas, with no existing accounts or local process. The targets were independent convenience, grocery, liquor and smoke shops that already worked with established distributors and weren’t looking to add vendors.

I positioned RepRally on what their current distributors couldn’t offer, walked in cold and moved owners onto an app-based ordering platform. I then expanded from convenience stores into wholesale accounts and retained the account base through the September 2025 Texas category ban.
