The role

  • Build your book. Prospect, demo, close and retain your accounts.
  • Full-cycle, in-person sales to restaurants, salons, contractors, clinics, gyms, retail and service businesses.
  • Build recurring revenue that compounds month over month.
  • Shape the playbook. Direct product feedback to the founder.

The comp

  • 1099, commission only, no base pay.
  • You set the price for each site.
  • You keep 90% of each account's first payment. If the account starts with the $1 first month, you also keep 90% of their first full month.
  • Then 20% of every monthly payment, rising to 25%, 30% and 35% as your lifetime accounts grow (25, 100, 250). Every raise covers your whole book.
  • One-time setup fees are split 50/50 with naber.
  • Free use of the app for the time on your invite (30 to 90 days), with monthly limits. naber tells you before it ends.
  • Titles and perks on the way: Rep at 10 accounts, Senior Rep at 50, Lead at 150.
  • Clients pay through Stripe, straight to you.
