hello@naberstudio.com

naber (app.naberstudio.com) is a new Austin startup. Reps sell websites and monthly online help to local businesses that are invisible online: taco trucks, barbers, salons, roofers, cleaners. You build the owner a real website before you walk in, show it to them on your phone, and sign them up on the spot.

What it costs you

Nothing. The tools and the training are free.

What you’ll do

  • Pick a part of Austin and own it as your territory
  • Find businesses with no website on the naber map
  • Build their site in one tap, then show it to the owner in person
  • Sign them up for a monthly plan and keep them happy

How you get paid

  • You’re a 1099 independent business owner, no base pay
  • You set your own price for setup and monthly service
  • Clients pay you through Stripe. naber takes 15% + $5 from each payment, only when you get paid
  • Example: a client on $49/mo pays you $36.65 every month they stay. 30 clients is about $1,100 a month

Who this is for

  • Students, recent grads, and anyone who likes talking to people face to face
  • People who’ve done door-to-door, retail, restaurant or field sales
  • Team leaders who want to build a territory and bring on their own reps
  • You need a smartphone and a way to get around Austin. No tech skills needed

Why join now

Founding reps get first pick of territories, and the top founding reps become territory leads with their own team.

How to apply

Email hello@naberstudio.com with your name, your phone number, the part of Austin you know best, and one line on anything you’ve sold before. I’ll reply within one business day.

Your income depends on what you sell and keep. There’s no cost to start.
