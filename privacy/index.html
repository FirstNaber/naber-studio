Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

Naber Studio is operated by Faris Naber (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect through naberstudio.com and our services, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, email, phone number and business details when you email us or become a client.
  • Project materials. Content you share for your website, such as text, photos, logos and product information.
  • Payment details. Payments are handled by Stripe. We don’t see or store your full card number; Stripe processes it under its own privacy policy.

What this website collects

naberstudio.com doesn’t use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare proposals and deliver the work you hire us for
  • To send invoices, receipts and service messages
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it except with the services we need to run the business (for example Stripe for payments, and email and hosting providers), or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep client and billing records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and tax requirements. You can ask us to delete information we no longer need to keep.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@naberstudio.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
